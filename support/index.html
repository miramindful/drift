Common questions

How do I restore Drift Plus?

Open Drift’s Settings, go to Drift Plus, and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account you used for the purchase. If access does not return, check that your subscription is active in your Apple Account and email us with the app version and any error message.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Drift. You can also use Manage subscriptions in Drift Plus. Access continues through the paid period after cancellation. Open Apple’s subscription settings ↗

Why didn’t a reminder arrive?

Check that notifications for Drift are allowed in iPhone Settings. Focus modes, notification summaries, and device settings may affect when you see a reminder. Reopen the drift to check its return date. Reminder delivery depends on iOS and is not guaranteed at an exact time.

Where are my thoughts stored?

Your entries are stored locally on your device. Drift does not require an account or send your thoughts to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include app data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete a drift?

Find the entry in All drifts or History and choose Delete drift. This removes its journey and reminders and removes it from Insights. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading retains data. Backups are managed separately through Apple.

What remains free?

Your saved drifts and history remain accessible without Drift Plus. Plus adds deeper analytics, including category patterns, monthly reflections, and longer activity trends.

How do refunds work?

Apple handles purchase refunds. You can request one through Apple’s Report a Problem website. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple under its policies.
