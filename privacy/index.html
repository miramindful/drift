Privacy Policy

Updated October 1, 2026

What Drift stores

Drift stores the thoughts you enter, their categories, capture and check-in dates, your check-in answers, outcomes, and activity history. Your default check-in preference is also saved. This information supports reminders, history, and the patterns shown in Insights.

Where your information stays

This version stores your information in the app's storage on your device. It does not require an account or include developer-operated servers, advertising, third-party analytics, or AI services that receive your thoughts. Insights are calculated on your device from your saved entries.

Optional purchases

Drift Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription processed by Apple. Existing one-time purchases remain supported. Drift checks Apple purchase records to unlock paid charts and restore access. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple handles purchase and payment information under its own policies.

Reminders

With your permission, Drift schedules local notifications through Apple's notification system. The reminder displays a generic message rather than your thought's text. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Retention and removal

Saved information remains in the app until its data is removed. You can delete any drift and its journey from All drifts, or previous drifts from History, using Delete drift. Its reminders are also removed. Deleting a drift also removes it from Insights. Deleting the app through iOS removes its local app data; offloading the app retains that data. Device backups may retain copies, and restoring a backup may restore app data. Manage backups separately through your Apple device or account settings.

Your device and Apple services

Device backups and any diagnostics you choose to share through Apple are handled under Apple's settings and policies. Protect access to your device. Drift does not promise that device storage or backups are immune to loss or unauthorized access.

Policy updates and contact

This policy describes the current version and will be updated if Drift's data practices change. Drift is developed by Danay Robinson, creator of Lumine. For privacy questions, contact lumine1431@gmail.com.

This website and support email

This website provides information and support for Drift. It does not ask you to enter your saved thoughts or create a Drift account, and we have not added advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the website.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive thoughts or payment information. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations.
